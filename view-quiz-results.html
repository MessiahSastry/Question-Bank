Students are displayed by section and roll number. Ranking is calculated across the entire class using 80% accuracy and 20% completion speed. Only the first recorded official attempt counts; practice attempts do not change ranks. Final Marks are out of the quiz total: 80% actual marks + 20% speed marks. Full speed marks for completion within 12 seconds/question (3 minutes for 15 questions); no speed marks at 60 seconds/question or longer. Rankings are provisional and may change as more students attempt the quiz. The school roster is checked to identify students who have not attempted.

Results are for authorised school staff. Student phone numbers are not shown in this report.
